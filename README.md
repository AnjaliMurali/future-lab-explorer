# Future Lab Explorers

Prompt: Design & Develop a Futuristic Interactive Science Experiment Platform for Kids (Ages 10–15)

Create a world-class, futuristic, immersive web application that feels like a combination of NASA Mission Control, Marvel's futuristic laboratories, high-tech sci-fi interfaces, and next-generation STEM education.

The platform should inspire curiosity by allowing children aged 10–15 years to safely perform "impossible" real-life science experiments through highly interactive simulations. Every experiment should teach scientific concepts through engaging visuals, animations, realistic physics, and age-appropriate explanations.

The overall experience should feel like entering a futuristic science laboratory where every click unlocks discovery.

Overall Goal

Create the most visually stunning, engaging, scientifically accurate, and inspiring online science laboratory for children aged 10–15. The platform should combine the excitement of a video game, the wonder of a science museum, the sophistication of a NASA control center, and the guidance of a friendly AI mentor into a single unforgettable learning experience that motivates students to explore, experiment, and discover through interactive simulations.
